Data Sources & Terminology

NWS Point Forecast: Written and adjusted by human expert meteorologists at local National Weather Service (NWS) offices.

MOS (Model Output Statistics): Raw statistical algorithms (GFS MOS and NAM MOS) that correct computer models based on historical station data. Viewing MOS vs NWS shows you the uncertainty spread.

ASOS/METAR: Highly accurate, automated ground weather sensors located at major airports for real-time truth.
